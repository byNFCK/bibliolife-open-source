# BiblioLife application artwork

The primary cat-on-book application artwork in `assets/icons/app_icon.png` was supplied and confirmed by the project owner as their own creation. The Android adaptive icon layers in `assets/icons/foreground.png` and `assets/icons/background.png` are project artwork derived from that master.

The iOS, macOS, web/PWA, Windows and favicon raster sizes in this candidate are generated exports of the same owner-confirmed master artwork, composited onto an opaque navy `#011D51` background for platform icon requirements. They are not Flutter template artwork. The platform asset catalogs/manifests retain the standard Flutter/Xcode template structure where applicable; their template origins are separately attributed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

Do not replace the owner-created master or claim ownership of any separately licensed third-party icon/font merely because it is included in the application.
